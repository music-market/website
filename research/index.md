# Research

The repositories were built for sociological research on cultural markets, with recorded music as the case. In such markets the quality of a product is uncertain and its value is established socially: by the charts that rank it, the juries that award it, the critics and communities that rate and classify it, and the listeners who follow one another's taste. The ten sources were chosen so that these processes can be observed on the same products and the same people.

## Four themes

- **Categories.** Genres are not given; they are proposed, voted on and revised. Rate Your Music records every proposal for a new genre, the votes for and against it, the discussion around it, and the votes that attach a genre to an album. Discogs and Spotify carry the genres that catalogues and platforms assign.
- **Evaluation.** Billboard charts record commercial success week by week since 1957. The Grammy Awards record who was nominated and who won, ceremony by ceremony since 1959. Rate Your Music and Resident Advisor record reviews and ratings. Together they let market, jury and critical judgements be compared on the same works.
- **Reuse.** WhoSampled traces which tracks sample which, down to the sampled element. The resulting network connects works across genres and decades and shows how new music is built from old.
- **Taste and ties.** SoundCloud exposes what listeners like, repost and comment on, and whom they follow, with timestamps. The tie-formation study follows a panel of users over weeks, detects the new following ties they form, and asks which candidate ties form and what a new tie does to the user's subsequent choices, with the atypicality of the new contact's likes as the key moderator.

## Design

The collections are built to support longitudinal and relational analysis rather than snapshots. Events carry their own timestamps where the source provides them, and where it does not, as for SoundCloud followings, formation timing is recovered by comparing recurrent snapshots. Identifiers are preserved so that a Spotify artist, a Grammy nominee and a MusicBrainz record can be linked, and sampling frames are recorded so that the population an estimate refers to is known.

## Status

Collection is at different stages across sources. Spotify, WhoSampled, the Grammys and Billboard were collected between 2019 and 2022, with Spotify extended by incremental and lyrics crawls since 2025. The SoundCloud profile and LIKES collections completed in mid 2026, the FOLLOWINGS collection in August 2026, and the COMMENTS collection is paused. Discogs is being refreshed from the March 2026 dump. Genius and Resident Advisor have crawlers but not yet documented data. Results are not reported on this site.
